San Diego's tiki bar scene has matured into something worth mapping. From North County's Pier View Way to Pacific Beach and downtown's craft cocktail corridors, the options are real and the quality level has risen over the last several years. Here's where to go in 2026.

## What San Diego's Tiki Scene Looks Like Now

The tiki revival that began in earnest in the 2010s has had time to settle in San Diego. The bars that survived did so by building rum programs from substance, constructing cocktail menus from the canon rather than the aesthetic, and finding their own identity within the tradition. The result rewards people who know what they're looking for.

## The Lobby Tiki Bar — Oceanside (North County)

**408 Pier View Way, Oceanside CA**

Start in North County. The Lobby Tiki Bar is the bar the rest of this list has to answer to. Inside The Brick Hotel on Pier View Way, steps from the Oceanside pier, it's running an extensive rum selection built into a cocktail menu that takes the tiki canon seriously.

The [menu](/menu) covers the full range — the Lobby Mai Tai is the house standard, the Cobra Kai (Jamaican pineapple pot still rum, Barbados rum, Falernum, absinthe) is the one to order if you want to commit, and the Painkiller comes in three levels of intensity. The Toucan Slam and Temple of Doom are both worth ordering if you're settling in for the evening.

[Happy hour](/happy-hour) daily specials make it one of the most accessible great cocktail bars in the county. Thursday's $6 Mai Tais are the headline; the full week runs rotating specials Monday through Friday. The island-inspired kitchen — Huli Huli Chicken, Poke Inari Bombs, Coconut Shrimp — is built to work alongside the rum program.

Open Tuesday through Sunday. [Reserve a table](https://tables.toasttab.com/restaurants/92e5306b-e932-4406-876b-91277626cb5c/findTime).

## Coco Cabana — Oceanside (Rooftop)

Also at The Brick Hotel, Coco Cabana is the rooftop complement to The Lobby's ground floor. More Caribbean-influenced, with open air, DJ nights on weekends, and views across Downtown Oceanside toward the coast. A natural two-stop itinerary in the same building.

## Pacific Beach

The Grass Skirt in Pacific Beach has been a reliable part of the SD tiki scene for years — a dedicated program with proper vessel service and a commitment to the rum-forward tradition. Worth the stop if you're in the beach communities.

## Shelter Island

Bali Hai carries the classic tiki restaurant tradition on Shelter Island, with views across San Diego Bay. It's more institution than discovery at this point, but the setting is genuine and the tradition is long. A different experience than the craft tiki bars — more about the full tiki restaurant history.

## Building a San Diego Tiki Itinerary

For a dedicated tiki bar experience — the rum program, the carved vessels, the full commitment — The Lobby in Oceanside is the current standard in the county. If you're building a day around it, Oceanside rewards the visit on its own terms: the pier, Pier View Way, the beach, and an evening anchored at The Lobby.
